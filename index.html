The Vibe Records story

From the underground to the world.

Vibe Records began in Miami in 1992 as a HOUSE-music label, shaped by the energy of the dance floor and the city's one-of-a-kind cultural mix.

More than three decades later, that independent spirit still drives us. Today, Vibe Records Miami reaches beyond any single genre—developing artists, producing original music and bringing together Caribbean, Latin, African and electronic influences.

1992

Vibe Records launches from Miami with HOUSE and underground Dance music at its core.
