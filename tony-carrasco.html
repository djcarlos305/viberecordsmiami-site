The Story

From the club floor
to the control room.

Tony Carrasco emerged from New York City's downtown club culture and carried that energy into the studios and dance floors of Europe, helping shape records that still move DJs decades later.

Born in New York City and raised on Manhattan's Lower East Side, Carrasco absorbed the language of underground disco early: extended mixes, 12-inch culture and the craft of building a room through sound.

Four decades of electronic music, from underground club culture to global dance floors.

With Mario Boncaldo as Klein & MBO, Carrasco helped create a run of electronic dance records including Dirty Talk, Wonderful and The MBO Theme.

Dirty Talk, released in 1982, became an enduring underground classic and is widely cited as an important bridge between post-disco, electro and early house music. The record has also been cited in discussions of the influences surrounding New Order's Blue Monday.

Across the decades, Carrasco has continued working as a DJ, producer, remixer and sound designer, with credits across house, electronic and Italo disco.
